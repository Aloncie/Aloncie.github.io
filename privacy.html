Privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy is about Youtube agent, a personal tool made and used only by Aloncie, the developer. What the app is.

What data the app uses

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for read-only access:

  • YouTube Data API (youtube.readonly): channel and video information, like titles and view counts;
  • YouTube Analytics API (yt-analytics.readonly): channel and video reports, like views, watch time and traffic sources.

The app can't upload, change or delete anything on YouTube.

How the data is used

Only to compare the statistics of my own channel with the numbers I collect from YouTube Studio.

Where the data is stored

The sign-in token and the reports stay on my computer and in my private repositories. I read them myself and with the AI assistant I use for my channel work. They are not sold, not used for ads and not shared with anyone else.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

How to remove access

You can remove the app's access at any time on the Google Account permissions page. To have stored data deleted, contact me.
